# Insuretech Connect (ITC)

> InsureTech Connect (ITC) is the world's largest insurance innovation event series, run by Clarion Events. Its flagship, ITC Vegas, gathers 9,000+ insurance carriers, insurtech startups, brokers, reinsurers, and investors for three days of content and networking, with regional editions in Europe and Asia. This is a company profile in the API Evangelist network; ITC is an events/media brand and does not currently publish a public developer API.
